# Daily use

## Waking Lola up

The robot starts asleep. Touch an **antenna gently** (it moves about 14 degrees) and it wakes up. If you switched on "start when the robot wakes up", Lola-Bla starts a few seconds later and greets in a short, warm sentence. Otherwise start it from Reachy Mini Control.

## Talking to Lola

She talks like a friend, not like a command list, in **Spanish, German or English**, whichever the child uses. A few things she does:

- **Hear messages.** Ask her to check messages. She asks which group, then plays the new ones in order and says who each one is from. A message is new until she has played it, whatever Signal says. You can stop her and continue later, or ask for one person's messages only.
- **Answer.** Ask her to send a message. She asks which group, says a few warm words, then a clear beep tells you when to speak. The recording stops after about five seconds of silence (30 seconds at most). She plays it back, and you choose: send it, try again, or throw it away. Before sending she says which group it goes to.
- **If something fails**, she says so. The recording is kept, so you can try again.
- **Everything else**, she keeps simple. If a child asks for something that is not for her, Lola gently says to ask a parent.

New messages are collected in the background every couple of minutes, so checking is quick.

## Sending Lola to sleep

Say "go to sleep", "duérmete" or "schlaf jetzt". She will **ask first**: "do you want me to go to sleep?" Only a clear yes ("yes", "sí", "ja") sends the robot to sleep, so playing or a half-heard word cannot switch her off. When it sleeps, the app stops and nothing is listening. Touch an antenna to wake it.

## The settings page

The settings are protected by your password, so they open in your **browser**, not inside Reachy Mini Control. From Control:

1. Open the Lola-Bla panel. It shows the Signal status (linked or not) and how to get to the settings.
2. Click the **arrow icon** at the top right of the panel. A page opens in its own window.
3. Press **open settings**. If nothing opens (that window cannot open links), press **copy** and paste the address into your browser.

What you can do there: link or unlink Signal, change your password, manage groups and contacts, read the activity and audit logs, and switch "start when the robot wakes up" on or off.
